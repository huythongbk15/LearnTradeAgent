`68b25945…`, and carried a **rule C** that said a change to the evidence
manifest is "bookkeeping". That rule was **wrong**: a change to a report, a
receipt or a verdict is a change to the evidence, not to its bookkeeping.

completed / failed, written durably per attempt) and records the scope token's
